    How it works

    Three simple steps turn a stack of statements into clarity — the AI does the reading, you stay in control.

    1

    Upload statements

    Drop PDFs, photos or scans — even split across files or years. Our AI (Gemini Flash) reads them natively, including messy scans.

    2

    AI extracts & you confirm

    Numbers land in an editable table with confidence flags. Unreadable cells are flagged, never guessed. Fix anything, then confirm.

    3

    Get the full picture

    From there, flow through analysis, forecast, ratios and a DCF valuation — each built from your confirmed numbers, explained in plain words.
